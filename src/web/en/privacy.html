What Le Larbin keeps about you

As little as possible. You can play without giving anything at all; the account is optional, and it asks for no e-mail address, no password and no real name.

Who is responsible

Le Larbin is an independent project. For any question about your data, or to exercise your rights: mickagames1@outlook.fr.

Without an account

Nothing is sent anywhere. Your player name, record, achievements, avatar and settings stay in your browser, which keeps them for you. Clearing the site’s data erases them.

During an online game, the server knows the name and avatar you chose, for the length of the game: they have to be shown to the other players. Nothing is kept afterwards.

The server also keeps anonymous counters, day by day: how many visits, games started and finished, shares, home-screen installs, and accounts created. It adds 1 to a total without recording who did it — no name, no identifier, no IP address. For a visit, it only records whether it came from a search engine, a shared link or a games portal — never the address of the page it came from. Your browser keeps the date of your last visit, so you are only counted once a day.

The daily challenge

When you finish the daily challenge, your browser sends the server the moves you played — not your score: the server replays the game to find it itself. It then keeps, for the day’s leaderboard, the name you play under, your points and your roles, visible to everyone. With an account, that’s your username; without one, your player name. These lines are erased after a month. So that a guest can only send one score a day, the server keeps a fingerprint of their IP address in memory until the next day — never the address itself, and never on disk.

With an account

The server keeps:

  • your username and avatar;
  • a fingerprint of your secret code — never the code itself, which is why nobody can send it to you again — and those of the sessions open on your devices;
  • your achievements, with their dates;
  • your record: for each game, the date, the mode (solo or online), the number of players, your place, your points and the number of rounds, plus the count of roles you got;
  • the result of your online games (date, win or not), used for the public leaderboard.

The leaderboard shows everyone your username, avatar, wins and online games. Nothing else is public.

What is never kept

  • no e-mail address, no password, no real name;
  • neither the cards you played nor what happens at the table;
  • not your IP address: the server only holds it for ten minutes, in memory, to limit repeated sign-in attempts and submissions.

Where, and for how long

The game page is served by Vercel, which measures its audience without cookies or personal identifiers. The game server and the accounts database are hosted by Northflank, in London. Like any host, they keep technical connection logs for their own security needs.

An account’s data is kept as long as the account exists.

Your rights

See: everything that is kept is shown in the game — your account, your achievements, your record. Erase: “Delete my account”, in the account panel, immediately erases the account, its achievements, record and results from the server, and frees the username. What is in your browser stays there until you clear its data.

For any other request — a question, a correction, a doubt — write to mickagames1@outlook.fr. You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL.
